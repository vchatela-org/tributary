What a refresh can't force

A device only sends its data when it syncs to its phone app: open the app first, then refresh. A source may also still be finishing what it holds (Google Health can keep a Fitbit night short for hours after the wake-up): the tool then fetches the short version, and the next refresh, once the source has finished, corrects it.

A tool is asked to fetch only when the deployment gave it a refresh hook (TRIBUTARY_FITBIT_GRAFANA_REFRESH_URL, TRIBUTARY_GARMIN_GRAFANA_REFRESH_URL); otherwise its data is read as it is, and its line above says how old that is. Each step waits at most {{ timeout | int }} s.
